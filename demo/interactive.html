Keeping the Referent in the DARK — interactive

Pick a case on the left. Click any line to drop it and watch the leak indicator recompute. Nothing is a mock: the lines are the real prompts, the answers are what the reader returned.

—

What to look for. In send it raw the account is visible and the reader answers — and the protected value is in the prompt. In compress and delete in place the account is gone or the record is gone, and the reader returns UNKNOWN. In DARK the account line survives as a stable alias, the question is rewritten to use the same alias, and the reader answers without the value ever appearing. Try dropping a line from DARK and watch which indicator turns red first.
The leak indicator is computed in this page by scanning the visible lines for any of the case's protected values, so it follows what you leave on screen rather than what was recorded. The answer indicator is the reader's recorded output for the original prompt; it does not change when you edit the lines, because altering them would require re-running the model, which this page cannot do.
